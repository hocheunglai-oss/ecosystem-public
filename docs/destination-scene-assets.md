# Retired transport harbour

The GP/MR/Aframax transport design was retired on 13 October 2026. Its vessel purchases, pricing, dispatch controls, voyage animation, transport history and transported-volume rewards are no longer part of ECO. The related implementation, sprite assets and transport-only tests were removed.

The current harbour uses a stationary Fratelli Cosulich bunker-barge illustration on desktop and mobile, with independent ambient ocean and lighthouse effects on desktop. The vessel is decorative and has no game controls or reward state.
