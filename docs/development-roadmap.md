# Development roadmap

## ECO 1.0 — SPC ready

Support the established SPC trading tasks with shared, authenticated persistence and verified access boundaries. Supplier profiles, Singapore comparison, country/port knowledge, enquiry parsing and review must remain consistent. Buyers and credit show `UNDER CONSTRUCTION: TO BE RELEASED WITH ECO 2.0` to non-administrators, including VIEW AS; real administrators retain access.

Keep countries and ports as the location model. Shared profile actions, tab management and private owner-only tabs use the same design language. Only administrators and SPC traders may change Singapore supplier information.

Fixture history uses the SPC column order and links to existing ECO users and supplier profiles. Preserve the original trading offices and source row provenance for the calendar 2025 and 2026 import. Administrators and SPC traders can read the shared history, and other members see fixtures they traded. Resolve ambiguous links before publication. Keep commercial values and the reconciliation outside public source, and retain existing browser fixture records in their owner’s workspace.

Closed suppliers retain their ECO identity and fixture links. Mark their status CLOSED in the supplier profile, show it in the ordinary supplier directory, and exclude closed suppliers from SPC VIEW before filtering results and pagination. Historical imports can use the same closed supplier identity.

### Fixture workflow

Reconcile and import the latest reviewed SPC sheet entries before implementing the new fixture workflow. Record the real signed-in buyer trader on the enquiry. At stem, the buyer trader selects an eligible supplier trader. The pending fixture appears in the assigned trader’s FIXTURES queue with a link to the original enquiry. Show the buyer the assignment and confirmation status. Allow pending reassignment and record who confirms the fixture.

Show one vessel summary row in FIXTURES with `DATE`, `SUPPLIER TRADER`, `BUYER TRADER`, `VESSEL`, `ETA`, `HSFO`, `VLSFO`, and `LSMGO` in that order. The row opens a details window where the assigned supplier trader selects the account and suppliers, records agreed quantities and buy prices, and confirms the fixture. Support several grades, suppliers, and buy prices on the same fixture, including multiple supply lines for one grade.

Keep the list compact and display trader first names with office codes, such as `LANA (SG)` and `LONG (HK)`. Put both trader and office filters in each trader column header. Show known IMOs in parentheses and put `CANCELLED` in red beside the vessel name. Omit the `FROM` prefix in the list. Double-click a row or use EDIT in its details to open the supply-line grid for the assigned supplier trader or an administrator. The editor exposes ETA FROM and ETA TO, and retains the real buyer and assigned supplier trader. Deletion hides the fixture from the active register and can be reversed. Retain private evidence for edits, cancellation, deletion and restoration. For imported fixtures, apply verified updates from the sheet to the affected fields and retain the earlier ECO edits in the audit.

Support an exact ETA, a date range, and an earliest ETA with no end date. Flag an ETA that precedes the fixture date for review. Treat the imported SPC earliest ETA as an earliest date when there is no end date in the source.

Use one summary row per vessel booking, with agreed quantities aggregated by grade and each supply line retained in the details window. Use a confirmed fixture or enquiry reference to group historical supply lines. Account choices come from the offices in Users & Access, excluding CREDIT. The supplier trader can apply an account to the fixture’s supply lines and select a different account on any individual line.

The historical fixture view shows the recorded quantities and ranges. Use confirmed quantities for actual-volume statistics. New fixture confirmation requires exact MT on each supply line and explicit review when a quantity falls outside the grade’s review limits.

Use the SPC Google Sheet as the source of truth for imported fixture records. Apply verified sheet amendments and retain the earlier values and source evidence in the private audit.

For reviewed SPC imports, keep the original row and source digest. An order crossed out in the sheet appears as CANCELLED in ECO with its original commercial details. For individual crossed-out values followed by a replacement, show the current vessel or quantity and retain the source evidence privately. Display both in plain text in FIXTURES. Count cancelled orders separately when fixture statistics are built.

Let the buyer trader cancel a pending STEM. After supplier confirmation, the assigned supplier trader or an administrator can cancel it. Record the reason, actor and time and retain the booking and its original supply details. Associate verified split bookings in legacy imports with one fixture row and keep each supplier, quantity, account and price in the details window.
