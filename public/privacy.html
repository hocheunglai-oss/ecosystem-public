ECO Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 October 2026

ECO processes business enquiries for the Fratelli Cosulich team. This notice covers information submitted to ECO, including messages sent or forwarded by email or WhatsApp.

Information and use

We process contact details, message content, documents and related records to receive, review and follow up business enquiries. Information is available to authorised staff and service providers supporting these purposes.

ECO uses sign-in cookies. AI processing is optional and is requested by an authorised user; receiving a message does not automatically send it to AI.

Records and requests

Records are retained for business follow-up and review. Reading or dismissing a notification does not delete the underlying record.

For questions or to request access, correction or deletion, email:

otto@cosulich.com.hk

Include enough information to identify the relevant record. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
