ECO Privacy Notice

Last updated 27 November 2026

Fratelli Cosulich Marine Energy uses ECO to handle business enquiries and fixtures. This notice covers ECO accounts and information supplied to ECO, including messages sent or forwarded by email or WhatsApp.

Information we collect and use

We receive account and work contact details from the staff directory, users and business correspondents. ECO also processes enquiries, attachments, fixture records and activity logs to operate the service, follow up transactions, manage access and investigate issues. A company account and credentials are required to sign in. If you choose to use WhatsApp intake, ECO needs your approved number to associate messages with your account.

Sharing and processing

Authorised staff and purchasing teams receive information for their work. Hosting, storage, backup and communications providers process information needed to deliver these services. Some providers process information in other countries. Receiving an enquiry does not automatically send it to AI. If an authorised user requests AI FIX or AI review, ECO sends the relevant enquiry or review material to an AI service.

ECO uses sign-in cookies and browser storage for preferences and local drafts. Staff can read the account guidance under Settings → Privacy.

Records and requests

We retain records while needed for trading, audit, security or applicable legal obligations. Clearing a notification hides the alert and keeps the underlying record.

For privacy questions and personal data access or correction requests, contact ECO’s privacy contact, Otto Lai. You may also ask for deletion. We assess requests against applicable law and retention requirements.
